About SignTec

Crafting Iconic
Signage Since 1996

We are Gauteng's leading signage and fabrication specialists, serving Pretoria, Johannesburg, and clients across South Africa. Our commitment to quality craftsmanship and innovative design has made us the trusted partner for businesses that demand excellence.

From concept to installation, our in-house team handles every detail. Whether you need a bold LED illuminated sign or precision CNC-cut aluminium lettering, we deliver work that stands out — and stands the test of time.

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive signage solutions — from concept through fabrication and installation.

01

3D Aluminium Signage

Bold, dimensional aluminium lettering and logos with premium brushed or powder-coated finishes.

02

3D Perspex

Custom-cut perspex lettering and shapes — illuminated or solid — for striking interior and exterior signage.

03

ACM Fabrication

Aluminium composite panels routed and fabricated into custom fascias, cladding, and signage structures.

04

Aluminium Cut Outs

Precision CNC-routed aluminium cut-outs for intricate logos and display applications.

05

LED Illuminated Signs

Energy-efficient LED channel letters and lightboxes that command attention day and night.

06

Neon Signage

Handcrafted neon and flex-neon signs that bring warmth, personality, and iconic glow to any space.

07

Vinyl Printing & Cutting

High-resolution vinyl graphics and precision die-cutting for vehicles, windows, and surface applications.

08

CNC Cutting & Fabrication

Computer-controlled precision cutting through metals, acrylics, and composites for complex shapes.

09

Flat-Bed Printing

UV flat-bed printing directly onto rigid substrates — boards, acrylics, metals — at any format.

10

Structural Approval

Full structural engineering sign-off services for large-format and pylon signage installations.

11

Custom Signage

Fully bespoke signage design and fabrication — if you can imagine it, we can make it.

12

Digital Printing

Large-format digital printing for banners, backlit graphics, trade show displays and more.
